What I learned

Bioinformatics training combining biological concepts with practical data analysis in Python, pandas, and R. We compared genomic data, explored relationships through correlation analysis, and studied how computational tools help interpret biological datasets.

Practical work

August 2024–March 2025 · In person. Practical exercises used public biological data from Saudi and international sources, including genomic and DNA-related datasets. The program focused on analysis exercises rather than a standalone capstone project.

Biology through data

We studied bioinformatics concepts alongside hands-on analysis of genome and DNA-related data. The exercises connected biological questions with computational comparison and exploration.

Python, pandas, and R

Python and pandas supported working with tabular datasets, preparing data for comparison, and exploring patterns. R was also used for studying and analyzing the data. Correlation analysis helped investigate relationships between measurements.

Learning from public datasets

Training used public biological data from Saudi and global sources. The emphasis was understanding the data, comparing it, and interpreting results in their biological context.
