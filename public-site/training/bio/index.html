Advanced Bioinformatics Training Program

What I learned

Training in biological concepts and practical data analysis with Python, pandas and R.

Practical work

August 2024–March 2025 · In person. Practical exercises used public biological data from Saudi and international sources, including genomic and DNA-related datasets.

Biology through data

Genome and DNA-related datasets connected biological questions to computational comparison and exploration.

Python, pandas, and R

Python and pandas supported preparing tabular data and comparing patterns. R was also used for analysis, and correlation analysis helped examine relationships between measurements.

Interpreting results

The emphasis was interpreting results in their biological context, supported by data comparisons and correlation analysis.
