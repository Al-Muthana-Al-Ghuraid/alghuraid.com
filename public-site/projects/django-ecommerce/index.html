Django E-commerce Platform

An end-to-end training project built during Tuwaiq Academy’s Django program. I developed the storefront and administration features, including accounts, product browsing, carts, coupons and order flow.

Conceptual diagrams of the implemented store features.

Store architecture

Shoppers use HTML, CSS and Bootstrap templates, while staff use Django administration. Both interfaces connect to shared relational data through the Django ORM.

Open diagram ↗

Shopping and cart flow

Customers browse categories or search products, review product information and ratings, then add items to the cart. Coupons and calculated totals are applied before continuing through the order flow.

Open diagram ↗

Store administration

Staff manage product details, prices, images, discounts, stock, visibility and customer reviews. Shared models cover categories, products, stock, carts, orders and reviews.
