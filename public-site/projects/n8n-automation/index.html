Job-search Automation on Oracle Cloud

Three workflows hosted on Oracle Cloud using Docker and n8n, with Cloudflare access, Google Sheets data and dashboards, and scheduled backups.

Job Discovery

Daily collection from four job boards, followed by normalization, duplicate checks, bilingual rules, and AI checks for ambiguous records. Matches and rejections are stored separately in Google Sheets.

Swipe to explore the diagram ↔Open diagram ↗

Lead Finder

Search settings drive Apify collection. Existing leads are checked before scoring and preparing messages. New leads are appended; both result branches produce a run log. Data and statistics live in Google Sheets.

Swipe to explore the diagram ↔Open diagram ↗

Recruitment Email

The n8n Schedule Trigger processes the list in weekly batches; manual execution is also available. Each batch retrieves the CV from Google Drive, sends through Gmail, updates sent status, and waits between messages.
