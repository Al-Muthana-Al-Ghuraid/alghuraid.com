Job-search Automation on Oracle Cloud

A personal automation platform for discovering jobs, finding relevant contacts and managing job-application emails. I designed and deployed the workflows on Oracle Cloud using Docker and n8n, with Cloudflare access, Google Sheets tracking and scheduled backups.

Job Discovery

The job-discovery workflow collects posts from four platforms, standardizes records and removes duplicates. Arabic and English rules handle clear cases; AI extracts information from ambiguous posts. Matches and exclusions are recorded separately in Google Sheets.

Lead Finder

The contact-discovery workflow uses search settings to collect leads through Apify, checks existing records, scores results and prepares messages. Both the new-lead and no-new-lead paths produce a run summary.

Open diagram ↗

Job-application emails

Job-application emails are processed in weekly batches, with manual runs available. Each batch retrieves the CV from Google Drive, sends messages through Gmail, records their status and pauses between sends.
